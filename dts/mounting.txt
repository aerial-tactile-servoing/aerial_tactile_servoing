FRAME AND MOUNTING CONVENTIONS

The implementation is in tk3_sim/core/utils.py. Distances are in meters and
angles passed to the transforms are in radians unless the name says DEG.

Rotations
- frame_R_basis maps coordinates from frame into basis.
- POM provides the body-to-world quaternion as qw, qx, qy, qz.
- SciPy Rotation receives the same quaternion reordered as qx, qy, qz, qw.
- The camera-to-sensor rotation is diag(-1, -1, 1).
- The sensor-to-body rotation is a -30 degree yaw about body z.
- The sensor origin is [0, 0, -d] in body coordinates, where
  d = (74.255 + 65.0) / 1000 = 0.139255 m.

Twists
- A twist is [vx, vy, vz, wx, wy, wz].
- For rotation R and origin offset p in the destination frame:
    v_destination = R @ v_source + p cross (R @ w_source)
    w_destination = R @ w_source
- camera_to_world_twist_matrix composes camera-to-sensor, sensor-to-body,
  then body-to-world transforms.

Current DTS controller
- The solved sensor command is [vx_sensor, vy_sensor, wz_sensor].
- Sensor vz, wx and wy are zero.
- After the sensor-to-body transform, body vz, wx and wy are also zeroed.
- The command is rotated into world coordinates, then world vz is zeroed.
- Altitude is sent as the uavpos position reference, separately from velocity.

Update SENSOR_YAW_WRT_BODY_DEG and DEFAULT_SENSOR_Z_OFFSET_M in utils.py when
the mounting changes, and use camera calibration for the same crop/resolution.
